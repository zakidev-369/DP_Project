# Evidence of relevance and user research

Published studies establish a research problem and limits of existing detection [R01, R08]. They do not establish demand for this particular application.

No completed interview or client agreement is recorded in this pack. Collect 3–5 genuine discussions with developers, designers and QA reviewers, or obtain a substantive agreement with an actual client. Record what changes as a result.

Ask for voluntary participation and permission for anonymised notes. Use participant codes in public summaries and keep identifiable notes private.

| Prompt | Evidence to record |
| --- | --- |
| Describe your latest interface review | Actual workflow, tools and a concrete difficulty. |
| What makes a flag worth investigating? | Evidence needed and examples of false alarms. |
| Try the supplied demonstration | Observed confusion, missing information and useful actions. |
| What result would you act on? | Acceptance conditions and responsibility for decisions. |
| What should this tool avoid? | Privacy and scanning restrictions. |

- Record date, role, consent, observations, authorised short quotes, findings and linked requirement IDs.
- Unvalidated hypotheses: users need region evidence (FR03), editable judgements (FR04) and export (FR05).
- Use planning/user_research_template.md. Preserve negative feedback and disagreements.
