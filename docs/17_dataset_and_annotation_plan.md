# Dataset and annotation plan

Target 400–600 regions from at least 30 registrable domains if feasible, including a substantial benign set. Begin with a 50-region pilot. These are plans, not collected data.

Record provenance, reuse conditions and capture settings. Public accessibility does not establish redistribution rights. Keep raw third-party captures outside the public repository until reuse and redaction are checked.

| Label | Operational rule | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Confirmshaming | Refusal option uses degrading or guilt-inducing language. | Neutral “No thanks” alone is negative. |
| Misleading choice wording | Wording or polarity obscures the consequence of selection. | Require surrounding context that explains the ambiguity. |
| Visual interference | One available choice is materially harder to notice. | Record relative size, placement and contrast; colour alone is insufficient. |
| Urgency cue | Countdown or time pressure encourages immediate action. | Does not prove the time limit is false. |
| No supported cue | None of the four labels is supported. | Does not certify the entire page. |

- Akbar annotates; Zaki independently reviews at least 20% before seeing initial labels. Record raw agreement and per-label Cohen’s kappa where defined, then adjudicate.
- Mark ambiguous examples uncertain and exclude from the primary scored set or report separately.
- Remove exact/near duplicates before splitting. Group by registrable domain and shared template family where known.
- Aim for 60/20/20 group splits with class coverage. Freeze manifests before evaluation.
