# Submission checklist and supervision

Prepared documents support analysis and design. They do not prove that interviews, supervision, peer review or experiments have occurred.

| Area | Prepared | Remaining action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Proposal | Aim, scope, roles and objectives | Team and supervisor confirmation |
| Relevance | Research synthesis and interview form | Real discussions or client agreement |
| Literature | 12-source analysis and bibliography | Team verifies understanding and relevant full texts |
| Comparisons | 4 systems × 8 criteria; 3 technology decisions | Feasibility checks |
| Requirements and architecture | Acceptance criteria and 3 diagrams | Peer and supervisor review |
| UX and evaluation | Offline demo and verification plan | Actual usability and implementation evidence later |
| Management | Owned backlog, risks and schedule | Real YouTrack project, assignments and access |
| Supervision | Feedback form | Actual comments and revisions |
| Repository | Proposed package and workflow | Genuine contributions, review and accepted submission tag |

- Confirm the submission time and channel with the university.
- Before tagging, open every deliverable, resolve or disclose gaps and merge accepted work.
- Create pre-defense-1 once on the accepted commit. Never move a submitted tag.
- Feedback log fields: date, deliverable, actual comment, decision, owner, revision link and unresolved issue. No approval or signature is supplied.
